Brex, Data Analyst II (San Francisco, hybrid)
Source: https://www.brex.com/careers/8463696002?gh_jid=8463696002 (public posting, retrieved 2026-10-20)
Summary written for this demo; read the original posting for the full text.

Brex sells corporate cards, banking and spend management software to companies. Its Data team builds insights, models and data infrastructure for Sales, Marketing, Product, Engineering and Operations.

The role:
- Build business intelligence dashboards in Looker and/or Hex that tell a clear story
- Run ad-hoc analyses and deep dives on business questions and recommend actions from the data
- Create self-service data tools so business teams can track their own performance
- Work with Data Scientists and Data Engineers on data sources, pipelines and analytical data models behind reports
- Set up and maintain data quality checks on the datasets that feed dashboards
- Partner with Sales, Operations, Product and Finance on reporting for planning
- Automate recurring analyses and reports in Python

Asks for 3+ years in data analytics or a related role.
