BILL makes accounts payable, receivable and spend software for small and mid-size businesses. This role sits on the Risk Portfolio Management and Strategic Programs team, which runs programs across credit, fraud and reputational risk.

The role:
- Own strategic project plans and use statistical and analytical methods on project data to spot performance patterns and early risk signals
- Build operating frameworks for risk initiatives and try automation and AI-assisted tools, such as LLM copilots, to cut manual work
- Design dashboards in Tableau, AWS QuickSight or similar tools for sponsors and leadership
- Manage third-party vendors: track performance metrics, evaluate them, monitor ROI and watch budget lines
- Keep cross-functional partners aligned on priorities
